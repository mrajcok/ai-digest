FAA expands AI advice to manage air traffic congestion across entire US airspace

The FAA is expanding the use of its SMART AI system to provide recommendations for managing air traffic across the entire US National Airspace System.

  • The SMART system, costing $875 million, uses cloud-based data including weather patterns, flight paths, traffic flow, and controller staffing to predict air traffic and recommend optimal routes and scheduling.
  • Since initial implementation on September 21 in the National Capital Region, SMART has reportedly rerouted 180 flights, saving an average of 10 minutes and 80 gallons of fuel per flight, according to the FAA.
  • FAA specialists validate SMART’s recommendations before they are passed to air traffic controllers, who retain final decision-making authority.
  • The system’s deployment has expanded to air route traffic control centers in Atlanta, Jacksonville, and Miami, with plans for nationwide coverage across all 22 centers.
