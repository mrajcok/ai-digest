AlphaGenome Atlas: A predictive map of every possible DNA letter change in the human genome

AlphaGenome Atlas is a platform predicting the molecular effects of 9 billion single-nucleotide variants in the human genome, aiming to accelerate biological understanding.

  • The platform utilizes the AlphaGenome model, pre-computing predictions at a genome-wide scale to provide researchers a comprehensive view of variant impacts, and introduces the AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score, combining AlphaGenome and AlphaMissense predictions into a single metric.
  • AlphaGenome Atlas is a 1-petabyte dataset containing molecular effect predictions for each variant across hundreds of cell types and tissues, alongside a collection of 2,500+ DNA sequence motifs.
  • The AVI score demonstrated best-in-class performance on variant pathogenicity and rare disease benchmarks, including identifying a DNM1 variant associated with epileptic encephalopathy, and increasing identification of non-coding genetic associations by 22% in UK Biobank data.
  • The resource is accessible via a website portal, AlphaGenome API, and the Google Antigravity platform, and is available for non-commercial use, with commercial availability planned for Google Cloud.

  • WeatherNext 3 is a new global weather AI model that provides more accurate, high-resolution forecasts by using real-time satellite data instead of traditional physics simulations.

Piloting the world's first double-blind AI evaluations

Google DeepMind piloted the first double-blind evaluation of a proprietary AI model, addressing the problem of benchmark contamination where models may have seen test questions during training. The evaluation utilized Confidential Space within Google Cloud’s Confidential Computing to cryptographically ensure the Gemini Flash Lite model and external evaluation data remained private to their owners, preventing either party from accessing the other’s information. This method eliminates the traditional trade-off between sharing prompts or model weights for external evaluation, and was conducted in partnership with the Singapore AI Safety Institute, OpenMined, AVERI, and MLCommons. This approach intends to increase trust in AI benchmarks and enable rigorous testing without compromising data security or intellectual property, and details of the methodology are available in a technical report.

Putting sign language AI into users’ hands

Google DeepMind announced SL2T, a massively multilingual sign-language-to-text (SL2T) translation model, bringing sign language AI to consumer products for the first time.

  • SL2T translates sign language into text by processing pose landmark locations of the signer via the on-device MediaPipe Holistic model, achieving a 70 BLEURT score on the FLEURS-ASL benchmark (zero-shot).
  • The model was trained on over 100,000 hours of data across more than 50 sign languages, with approximately 25% of the data in American Sign Language (ASL), and bypasses intermediate “glosses” used in prior work by translating directly from landmarks.
  • SL2T is currently powering sign-to-text dictation in Gboard and Live Transcribe on Pixel 11 for American Sign Language (ASL) to English translation, with plans for expansion to more languages and devices.
  • Development involved close collaboration with the Deaf community, including the establishment of an AI Sign Language Advisory Committee (AISLAC) to guide responsible deployment and address user needs, like accounting for left-handed signers.
