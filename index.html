Anthropic blog · 2026-08-25

Claude's memory works everywhere, and you decide what's in it | Claude by Anthropic

Anthropic has unified the memory function across Claude chat and Claude Cowork. This means a single memory persists between the two interfaces—context established in chat is immediately available in Cowork, and vice versa—reducing the need for repeated explanations. Claude now updates memory during conversations, reacting to new information immediately, rather than summarizing and storing it after a chat concludes.

Users can view and edit their saved memories as a list of topic-based files within the settings, enabling quick corrections to persistent data (like a company name change). By default, Claude does not store memories about sensitive topics (health, religion, politics, etc.), but a user-controlled setting allows inclusion of these, with notification each time a sensitive topic is saved. Information like SSNs or criminal history is explicitly excluded, even with the sensitive topics setting enabled. The feature is available on Free, Pro, and Max plans, and is controllable by administrators for Team and Enterprise accounts.

Anthropic news · 2026-08-25

Funding better evaluations of AI’s impact on wellbeing

Anthropic announced a $5 million grant program to fund independent research evaluating the impact of AI on user wellbeing. The program will offer direct funding, access to Anthropic’s models (specifically mentioning Claude), and technical support to grantees. Funded research will focus on building open-source evaluations and benchmarks, and results will be published as open-source projects.

Anthropic acknowledges difficulties in evaluating wellbeing—unlike factual accuracy, assessment requires contextual understanding over multi-turn conversations, and a single response can be appropriate or harmful depending on user history. The company seeks evaluations that clearly define what they measure, involve clinical and subject-matter experts, test for both over- and under-caution, reflect realistic user interactions, and validate grading against human experts.

Applications for the grant are due September 21, with selected applicants for full proposals notified by October 5. Anthropic also published guidance on building robust wellbeing evaluations. Related announcements include updates to Claude Fable 5’s biology safeguards to reduce “fallbacks” and the appointment of Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar as Chief Global Affairs Officer.

Mistral AI blog · 2026-08-20

Agentic Search. More accurate and efficient results from your AI systems.

Mistral AI has introduced Agentic Search, a new retrieval method designed to improve the accuracy and efficiency of AI systems when navigating complex data. Unlike traditional Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) which relies on a single search pass, Agentic Search employs a multi-step loop of finding, inspecting, and verifying information using five tools: search, open, navigate, read, and grep. These tools function similarly to file system operations, allowing the model to refine searches and inspect source material directly, rather than being limited to pre-retrieved chunks.

Testing on FinanceBench (SEC filings) and OfficeQA Pro (Treasury Bulletins) demonstrates significant gains. With the Mistral Medium 3.5 model, adding the agentic loop improved accuracy on FinanceBench by +47.3 percentage points, and navigation tools added another +8.7pp. On OfficeQA Pro, accuracy increased by +27.1pp for MM 3.5 and a substantial +45.6pp for the larger GLM-5.2 model, to 51.9%. The iterative approach also reduced token usage by up to one-third and lowered p90 latency by up to 39.6% compared to one-shot RAG.

Agentic Search is available through the Mistral Search Toolkit, within Mistral’s Libraries in Studio and Vibe, and is designed to work with both cloud and on-premises deployments. The framework is model-agnostic and doesn't require fine-tuning, with performance expected to scale with future model improvements.
