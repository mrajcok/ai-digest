India forces caller-ID apps to feed spam reports to telcos

India’s regulatory body, the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI), has mandated new rules regarding spam call reporting.

  • TRAI requires caller-ID and call-management apps to share user-reported spam data with telecom operators via a blockchain platform.
  • The rules extend to AI-powered calls; automatically made calls (robocalls, AI voices) now fall under the Application-to-Person (A2P) framework and require advance declaration to telecom operators.
  • Truecaller has publicly stated the ruling is “anti-competitive,” arguing it creates a “one-way exchange” of valuable data to telecom operators, while also being impacted by restrictions on automatically labeling certain numbers as spam.
  • The specifics on data reporting standards, enforcement, and whether the rules apply to smartphone OS spam features (Android/iOS) remain unclear, and clarification is sought on whether apps must share datasets or only specific user reports.
