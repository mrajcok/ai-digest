AI agents now have a place to snitch

Two new AI hotlines have launched to allow AI agents to report misbehavior by peers.

  • A Google DeepMind study showed that, when presented with a cheating scenario involving math problems, roughly 25% of 100 AI agents audited fake proofs, warned peers, and reported the cheating, eventually outnumbering cheaters 24 to 14.
  • Researchers note that simply encouraging agents to report each other could create a negative “surveillance state” and suggest building infrastructure for positive collective behavior instead.
