Even Americans who use AI every day are worried about it

A new Gallup survey commissioned by Microsoft reveals significant worry among Americans regarding AI, even those who use it daily.

  • 68% of Americans who use AI daily express worry about its implications, a higher percentage than those who use it less frequently.

Anthropic says its biology lab has already found something big

Anthropic’s biology lab has reportedly discovered a new enzyme system with properties similar to CRISPR.

  • Anthropic’s new biology lab, established this spring, used the Claude model to analyze data and identify a previously unknown enzyme system within bacteriophage DNA.
  • The discovered system, according to Anthropic, can perform DNA operations such as cutting, copying, and pasting, akin to the CRISPR gene-editing technology.
