OpenAI’s chief scientist has thoughts on how to limit AI development

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki proposed using “perplexity” – a measure of how well a model predicts the next token – as a benchmark to measure and potentially limit AI development. According to the post, perplexity has remained a consistent measurement of progress while other benchmarks quickly become outdated. Pachocki notes that GPT-4 had a perplexity of ~0.91 and recent models have achieved losses below 0.8, correlating with "transformative capabilities" in fields like cybersecurity and mathematics. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman described the post as “the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while,” suggesting the company is seriously considering perplexity as a shared standard for establishing limits on AI progress. The goal would be to halt development past a specific perplexity threshold until improved understanding and monitoring of AI behavior is achieved.
