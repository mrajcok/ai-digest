OpenAI has introduced a new “Locked Chats” feature to ChatGPT, designed to provide privacy for sensitive conversations.

  • Users can secure conversations with a four-digit PIN or biometric authentication (Face ID or Touch ID on supported devices).
  • Locked chats are stored in a separate “Locked Chats” section, distinct from regular chat history, and have their own dedicated memory that doesn’t affect regular ChatGPT Memory.

Hugging Face’s policy chief, Irene Solaiman, discussed the challenges with current AI model evaluations at TechCrunch Disrupt.
