Apple launches new app to study how people use AI for health

Apple has launched a research study app, developed with OpenEvidence, to investigate user interaction with AI for health-related inquiries.

  • The app is available to U.S. users aged 21+ using iPhone 15 or later, running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence enabled, and aims to enroll up to 200,000 participants for over 12 months.
  • The app provides answers to health questions sourced from clinical data, including publications from The New England Journal of Medicine, Journal of the American Medical Association, and Nature, and aims to offer simplified explanations.
  • Users are able to share HealthKit data (resting heart rate, activity, sleep, lab results) for personalized suggestions and the app is designed to aid in preparing for doctor's visits and guide users to appropriate healthcare providers.
  • Data is anonymized and stored with a third-party cloud provider; query and response data is deleted after 24 hours and will not be used to train AI models, according to Apple.
