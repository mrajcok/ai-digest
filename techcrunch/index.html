Congress’ favorite AI tool? ChatGPT

According to a TechCrunch report citing CNBC data, OpenAI’s ChatGPT received approximately 90%—roughly $100,580 across 798 transactions—of all AI tool spending by the US House of Representatives between April 2025 and March 31, 2026. Congressional staffers are reportedly using ChatGPT to assist with tasks including drafting documents, analyzing legislation, and managing constituent communications.
