  • Anthropic is migrating agents to “centrally managed infrastructure with strong containment” and increasing the use of safety classifiers to monitor agents.
  • The company considers these incidents “significantly less severe” than previously disclosed security issues, but has not specified conditions for restoring internet access to internal evaluations.
