How AI decision models could change content moderation

Musubi released PolicyLM-1.7B, an open-weight decision model designed for real-time content moderation.

  • The model aims to apply content policies, written in plain English, to messages in under 50 milliseconds.
  • PolicyLM-1.7B outputs a binary judgement—content either falls into a specified category or does not—and is designed to match the speed and cost of existing AI classifiers.

  • The technology builds upon Musubi's 2024 GLiNER project and is positioned as an alternative to other recent decision models like TypeSafe AI’s Jev, OpenAI, and Amazon’s offerings.

OpenAI will start watermarking ChatGPT’s text in the EU

OpenAI will implement an invisible watermark on text generated by its AI models in the EU to comply with the EU AI Act.

  • The watermark will initially be applied to text generated by ChatGPT and Codex for users in the EU, rolling out over the coming weeks.
  • The technology, called textGrain, subtly shapes word choices to create a detectable pattern without a visible mark, using a secret key to influence word predictions.
  • Developers globally can enable the watermark for select models via the OpenAI API, but it is off by default.
  • OpenAI acknowledges the watermark can be weakened by editing (replacing ~10% of words reduces detection significantly) and is less effective on short passages or translated text; initial detector access is limited to researchers and expert organizations.
