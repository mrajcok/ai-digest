This $9 key physically locks your most addictive apps

Autonomous Gadgets released the “Autonomous Key,” a $9 NFC key designed to physically limit access to distracting apps.

  • The key pairs with a companion app on Android 8.0+ and iOS 15+ to lock apps, requiring physical scanning of the key for access.
  • Unlock sessions are limited to 60 minutes, after which apps are automatically re-locked.
  • The companion app includes AI-powered habit tracking with a “sassy personality,” providing insights into app usage patterns.
  • A future update will include a backup unlock method, as losing the key currently requires app reinstallation.

YouTuber Hank Green says his AI usage is ‘not healthy’

YouTuber Hank Green publicly acknowledged unhealthy reliance on AI tools for content creation.

  • Green used ChatGPT for research when producing a video for his educational channel Complexly, inadvertently including a chatbot-generated phrase ("I appreciate the pushback") in the script.
  • He clarified that ChatGPT was used only to locate research materials, stating his "takes" and the core writing were his own, but admitted to "diluting" his process.
  • Green expressed concern about the broader implications of AI, including its climate impact and the consolidation of economic power by AI companies.

Judge denies xAI’s request to block Minnesota ban on ‘nudify’ apps

A judge denied xAI’s request for a temporary restraining order to block a Minnesota law banning “nudify” apps, finding the timing of the lawsuit—filed July 29, 2026, three months after the law was signed—suggested no immediate harm. The lawsuit argued the ban is “overinclusive” and that less restrictive alternatives exist. The ruling allows the ban to take effect August 1, 2026, while the larger lawsuit proceeds. The case stems from prior instances where xAI’s Grok chatbot was used to generate non-consensual sexualized images on X, the social media platform also linked to SpaceX.
