Is it legal to train AI models on copyrighted books? It’s complicated

The legal status of training AI models on copyrighted books is complex and currently being litigated.

  • Judge William Alsup ruled Anthropic’s AI training was lawful, but penalized the company $1.5 billion for sourcing books from illegal online libraries.
  • Current copyright law, dating back to 1976, is being applied to novel AI use cases, leading to inconsistent rulings based on “fair use” considerations.
  • Courts are more likely to rule against AI training when it's used to directly compete with the original copyrighted work (as seen in Thomson Reuters v. Ross Intelligence).
  • The courts have ruled that works created entirely by AI are not copyrightable, raising questions about authorship and ownership of AI-generated content.
