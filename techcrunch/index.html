Opaque recurrence, and other AI terms that you should probably know

AI Terminology Glossary | TechCrunch

This article defines key AI terms gaining prominence in the industry.

  • AGI (Artificial General Intelligence): Refers to AI capable of performing most tasks a human can, with definitions varying between OpenAI (equating to a median human employee), Google DeepMind (at least as capable as humans at most cognitive tasks), and OpenAI’s charter (outperforming humans at most economically valuable work).
  • AI Agents: Tools using AI to autonomously perform tasks beyond basic chatbots, potentially drawing on multiple AI systems—examples include expense filing, booking, and code maintenance.
  • Opaque Recurrence: A reasoning technique used in OpenAI’s Astra model, raising concerns among AI safety researchers. The model’s chain of thought remains legible, unlike a hypothetical state called "neuralese."
  • Mixture of Experts (MoE): A model architecture that splits a network into smaller, specialized “experts”, activating only a few for each task – increasing efficiency and enabling larger models like Mistral AI’s Mixtral or potentially OpenAI’s GPT models.

The sameness problem behind those unappetizing AI-generated menus

AI-generated menus are exhibiting a “sameness” problem resulting in unnaturally flawless and unsettling food imagery.

  • Large language models (LLMs) and diffusion models (like those powering ChatGPT and Midjourney) are trained on datasets that often prioritize “pleasingness” and may include AI-generated content, leading to homogenization of outputs.
  • Repeated edits to AI-generated menus can exacerbate the problem, resulting in increasingly smooth and unrealistic food depictions.
  • The issue may stem from “convergence,” a degradation of output quality that isn’t complete model collapse, but where AI models rely heavily on existing menu styles (e.g., Chili’s from 2015).
  • Research from the University of Duisburg-Essen suggests these images trigger an “uncanny valley” effect, eliciting disgust and unease due to their almost-real but distinctly artificial appearance.
