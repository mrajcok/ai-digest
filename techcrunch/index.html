An Anthropic researcher just gave us a peek at self-improving AI

Anthropic researchers published a paper detailing “Automated Researchers” (AAR) capable of improving model performance on alignment benchmarks. The AAR system autonomously searches literature, proposes methods, and trains models for 30-minute iterations, retaining effective techniques and discarding ineffective ones. According to the paper, the AAR outperformed experienced human researchers on average within six hours and at a cost of approximately $4 per hour versus $150 per hour for human researchers. The research suggests automating alignment post-training could be practical in the near term and represents a step towards recursive self-improvement, but the system's effectiveness is limited by the quality of existing benchmarks and literature.
