India’s regulatory body, the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI), has mandated new rules regarding spam call reporting.

  • TRAI requires caller-ID and call-management apps to share user-reported spam data with telecom operators via a blockchain platform.
  • The rules extend to AI-powered calls; automatically made calls (robocalls, AI voices) now fall under the Application-to-Person (A2P) framework and require advance declaration to telecom operators.
