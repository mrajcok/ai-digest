Who’s really in charge at Anthropic after the IPO?

Anthropic’s corporate governance structure prioritizes AI safety and long-term benefit over typical shareholder control.

  • Anthropic is structured as a public benefit company, legally allowing it to prioritize AI safety alongside profit, protecting the board from lawsuits related to prioritizing safety.
  • A Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT), chaired by Neil Shah, controls a class of stock allowing it to elect a majority of the board (Class T directors). The LTBT currently has one vacancy.
  • Anthropic's seven founders collectively own a special class of stock giving them 50.1% of shareholder voting power, allowing them to elect three board members; this control is temporary, ending after ten years post-IPO or when fewer than three founders remain.
  • The board can overrule the LTBT on certain director nominations by putting the decision to a company-wide employee vote, potentially granting employees significant influence.
